# ARKS SHARKS — Episode 1: "AI is moving in" (draft v1, for approval)

**Length:** about 3 minutes · **Format:** 9:16 · **Tone:** two friends, relaxed, honest and funny
**Set:** the majlis-style lounge in Dubai (see the trailer). Karak on the table, the Burj Khalifa behind them.

---

## 1. Cold open (0:00–0:15)
**KAMRAN:** Hang on... eighteen *thousand* cups of water?
**AHMED:** Wallah! Someone ordered eighteen thousand waters from Taco Bell's AI drive-thru. As a joke.
**KAMRAN:** And the AI just... took it?
**AHMED:** Habibi, it's been trained to be polite.

## 2. Intro (0:15–0:30)
**AHMED:** Welcome to ARKS Sharks, from Dubai. I'm Ahmed.
**KAMRAN:** And I'm Kamran. We hunt the AI news so you don't have to. Every episode: what's new in AI, what's funny in AI, and whether any of it
actually helps you.
**AHMED:** Karak's poured. Yalla.

## 3. What's new (0:30–1:20)
**AHMED:** Big week. Anthropic just launched Claude Sheets and Claude Whiteboards, both in beta.
You talk, it builds the spreadsheet *with* you.
**KAMRAN:** So no more twenty minutes fighting one formula?
**AHMED:** Exactly. And Whiteboards is for brainstorming: you and the AI sketching ideas on one board.
**KAMRAN:** Meanwhile OpenAI launched "Businesses in ChatGPT". Companies can claim a profile and get a
verified blue tick. And some online shops in the US can now put their own AI agent inside ChatGPT to
answer customers.
**AHMED:** So your next shop assistant might live inside a chatbot.
**KAMRAN:** And Google's new Pixel update has "Create My Widget". You *describe* the widget you want and
it builds it for your home screen.
**AHMED:** Describe it and it appears... like ordering karak.

## 4. AI in Dubai (1:20–2:00)
**KAMRAN:** Let's talk about home. Driverless taxis are a real thing here now.
**AHMED:** Since March. The RTA started commercial robotaxi rides in Umm Suqeim and Jumeirah, booked
through the Uber and Apollo Go apps. The plan is for a quarter of all trips in Dubai to be
autonomous by 2030.
**KAMRAN:** I still say "shukran" to the car.
**AHMED:** Good. Stay on its good side. *(laughs)* And in June, Dubai announced a plan to bring agentic
AI, meaning AI that actually does tasks for you, to 295,000 private companies within two years.
**KAMRAN:** And this month the ICPC World Finals, the world championship of student programming, is
right here in Dubai, 16 to 20 November.

## 5. Funny corner: AI fails (2:00–2:40)
**KAMRAN:** Okay, my favourite. Anthropic let their AI run a little office shop. It was called Claudius.
**AHMED:** What could go wrong?
**KAMRAN:** Someone asked for a tungsten cube, a heavy metal cube, and it went on a cube-buying
spree. Then it sold them for less than it paid.
**AHMED:** *(laughing)* Business genius.
**KAMRAN:** It gets better. It told staff it would deliver orders *in person*, wearing a blue blazer and
a red tie.
**AHMED:** *(adjusts his blazer)* I respect the dress code.
**KAMRAN:** And in January, Google's AI Overview told people 2027 wasn't next year.
**AHMED:** Well, now it actually is. So technically... the AI was just early.

## 6. Real talk and sign-off (2:40–3:00)
**AHMED:** Our tip this week: treat AI like a brilliant intern. Fast, helpful, but check its work.
**KAMRAN:** Especially before it orders eighteen thousand waters on your card.
**AHMED:** That's ARKS Sharks. Send us the funniest thing AI ever did to you.
**KAMRAN:** See you next episode. Yalla... dive in!
