# ARKS SHARKS — Trailer (draft v2, for approval)

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, about 35–40 seconds, fast cuts
**Hosts:** Ahmed and Kamran, two friends chatting about AI
**Set:** a cosy majlis-style lounge in the Dubai studio at dusk: two armchairs angled towards each other,
a low table with karak tea glasses and dates, warm lamps, the Burj Khalifa through the window,
and an AI screen glowing softly in the background
**Voices:** Ahmed's and Kamran's own Cartesia voices, UK English
**Hook rule:** something absurd in the first 3 seconds, a new cut every 2–4 seconds, and a reason to
come back at the end

---

### Shot 1 — THE HOOK (0:00–0:04)
*Smash-cut straight into a close-up of Kamran, karak glass frozen halfway to his mouth.*

**KAMRAN:** Eighteen *thousand* cups of water?!
**AHMED:** *(leaning into frame)* From an AI drive-thru. And that's not even the craziest one.

### Shot 2 — TITLE SLAM (0:04–0:08)
*Fast push in from the Dubai skyline at dusk. A glowing blue holographic shark made of data particles
swims past the Burj Khalifa, then the camera bursts through the window into the lounge.
Bass hit. Title added in the edit: **ARKS SHARKS***

### Shot 3 — WHO WE ARE (0:08–0:14)
*Two-shot, both relaxed in the armchairs, grinning at the camera.*

**AHMED:** Welcome to ARKS Sharks. I'm Ahmed.
**KAMRAN:** I'm Kamran. We hunt the AI news...
**AHMED:** ...so you don't have to.

### Shot 4 — RAPID FIRE (0:14–0:22)
*Three snappy close-ups, one line each, with a whoosh between cuts.*

**AHMED:** Claude builds your spreadsheets now.
**KAMRAN:** ChatGPT's handing businesses a blue tick.
**AHMED:** And in Dubai? The taxi drives itself.

### Shot 5 — THE FUNNY BIT (0:22–0:31)
*Kamran leans in, telling a story. Ahmed is already laughing.*

**KAMRAN:** One AI ran an office shop. It bought a fridge full of metal cubes, then offered to
deliver them in person. In a blue blazer and a red tie.
**AHMED:** *(straightening his own blazer)* Honestly? Great taste.

### Shot 6 — SIGN-OFF (0:31–0:38)
*Both raise their karak glasses to the camera. The holographic shark swims across the screen behind them.*

**KAMRAN:** New features. Funny fails. Real talk.
**AHMED:** ARKS Sharks. Yalla... dive in.
*(Both laugh. End card added in the edit: **ARKS SHARKS — EPISODE 1 SOON**)*
